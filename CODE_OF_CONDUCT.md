# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone who contributes to InkBoard, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Standards and Expectations

We expect all community members and contributors to:
- Exercise empathy, kindness, and respect in all interactions.
- Provide and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and project.
- Respect differing viewpoints and life experiences.

Unacceptable behavior includes:
- Harassing, derogatory, or discriminatory language or behavior.
- Personal attacks or trolling.
- Publishing private information without explicit consent.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement and Reporting

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainers at `ellaboevans@gmail.com`. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with confidentiality.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate corrective action in response to any violations.
